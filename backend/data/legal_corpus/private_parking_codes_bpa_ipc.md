# Private Parking Code of Practice & ATA Codes (BPA / IPC)

Private car parks (supermarkets, retail parks, private landlords) are not
enforced by councils - a "Parking Charge Notice" from a private operator is a
contractual claim for breach of the parking terms (not a statutory PCN), and
is only enforceable if the operator is a member of an Accredited Trade
Association (ATA): the British Parking Association (BPA) or the
International Parking Community (IPC).

## Key points for an appeal

- **Grace periods**: ATA codes require a minimum ~10-minute grace period at
  the end of a stay (to leave) and a consideration period on entry
  (commonly 5 minutes) to decide whether to stay and pay, before a charge
  can be lawfully issued.
- **Signage standards**: entrance and bay signage must clearly and
  prominently display the terms (charge amount, restrictions) - unclear or
  insufficient signage undermines the contractual basis for the charge
  entirely, since there is no valid offer/acceptance of parking terms.
- **Keeper liability**: under Schedule 4 of the Protection of Freedoms Act
  2012, a private operator can only pursue the registered keeper (rather
  than needing to prove who was driving) if it follows a strict notice
  procedure and timeline; a defective Notice to Keeper breaks keeper
  liability, leaving the operator unable to pursue anyone but the actual
  driver.
- **Not a criminal or civil-enforcement debt**: because this is a private
  contractual claim, non-payment cannot lead to bailiffs at the doorstep
  without the operator first winning a County Court case - it is often
  much weaker in practice than a council PCN, and many operators drop
  claims when challenged via the ATA's own appeals service (POPLA for BPA,
  IAS for IPC).

## How Breathing Room uses this

The RAG engine treats "private parking operator" issuer type as a materially
different (weaker, contractual, not statutory) category from council PCNs,
and drafts appeals citing grace-period/signage/keeper-liability defects to
the operator's own ATA-mandated appeals service.
