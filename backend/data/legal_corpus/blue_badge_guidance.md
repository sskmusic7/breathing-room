# Blue Badge Scheme Local Authority Guidance (England)

Statutory guidance to local authorities on operating the Blue Badge disabled
parking permit scheme, including eligibility (automatic and
discretionary/"hidden disabilities" routes covering conditions that cause
very considerable psychological distress or risk of serious harm managing a
journey), permitted concessions, and enforcement discretion.

## Key points

- Blue Badge holders get specific parking concessions (e.g. free/extended
  on-street parking, exemption from certain loading restrictions) that vary
  by local scheme - a PCN issued to a vehicle displaying a valid badge used
  correctly is often void from the outset.
- The 2019 eligibility reform explicitly extended the "hidden disabilities"
  category to include people with severe anxiety, autism, and similar
  non-physical conditions where travel poses a serious risk.
- Misuse/incorrect display (wrong badge, expired badge, not the badge
  holder travelling) is a separate, real enforcement risk the RAG engine
  should not paper over - it should only draft a representation where the
  badge was validly held and correctly displayed.

## How Breathing Room uses this

Cross-referenced whenever a user indicates they hold, or would likely
qualify for, a Blue Badge, to check whether the specific contravention
should have been exempted or discretionarily waived under the local
authority's own published policy.
