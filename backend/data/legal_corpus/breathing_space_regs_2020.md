# Debt Respite Scheme ("Breathing Space") Regulations 2020

Gives a person in problem debt a legal right to a temporary freeze on most
creditor enforcement action, interest, fees, and contact, so they can get
proper debt advice without the situation escalating further.

## Key mechanics

- **Standard Breathing Space**: up to 60 days, must be arranged by a
  qualified debt adviser (not self-certified), available once every 12
  months per debt in general.
- **Mental Health Crisis Moratorium**: available for anyone receiving
  crisis mental health treatment, lasts for the duration of the crisis
  treatment plus 30 days, arranged by an Approved Mental Health
  Professional rather than a debt adviser, and has no 12-month limit.
- **Effect**: most creditors (including HMRC and local authorities for
  council tax) must stop charging interest/fees, pause active enforcement
  (including bailiff/enforcement agent action), and stop most contact
  attempting to collect the debt, for the duration of the moratorium.
- **Exclusions**: certain debts (e.g. some fines imposed by a court for an
  offence, child maintenance, some student loans, some deposits) are only
  partially covered and specific carve-outs apply - always flag rather than
  assume full coverage.

## How Breathing Room uses this

This is a signposting-only feature: because arranging a Breathing Space
requires a regulated debt adviser (or AMHP for the mental health route), the
app can explain the moratorium and its effect and generate a referral letter
to National Debtline/StepChange/Citizens Advice, but must not present itself
as able to grant or arrange it.
