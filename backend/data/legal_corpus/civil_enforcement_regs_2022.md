# Civil Enforcement of Road Traffic Contraventions Regulations 2022

This statutory instrument consolidates and updates the detailed procedural
rules that sit underneath TMA 2004 Part 6 for England: how PCNs must be
served, the prescribed form/content of notices, statutory response
timelines at each stage (Notice to Owner, Charge Certificate, Order for
Recovery), and the charge certificate uplift (commonly a 50% increase) that
applies if a PCN is not paid or challenged within the Notice to Owner
window.

## Key points for an appeal

- Prescribes the exact wording and information a PCN/NTO must contain; a
  defective notice (wrong date, missing statutory wording, wrong
  registration) is itself a ground for cancellation under procedural
  impropriety.
- Sets the statutory response windows that follow an NTO if the recipient
  does nothing: escalation to a Charge Certificate (usually after 28 days),
  then registration as a debt at the County Court (Order for Recovery) if
  still unpaid, which is the point enforcement agents (bailiffs) can
  become involved.
- Governs use of approved devices (CCTV/ANPR cameras) for enforcement,
  including certification requirements the council must meet for camera
  evidence to be valid.

## How Breathing Room uses this

Used alongside `tma_2004_part6` to pinpoint exactly which statutory stage a
scanned notice is at (from the wording and dates extracted by OCR) and
